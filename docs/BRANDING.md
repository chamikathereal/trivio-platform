# Trivio Brand Identity & Design System

## 1. Brand Overview

- **Product Name:** Trivio
- **Public Brand Domain:** `trivio.ink`
- **Positioning:** Payment Settlement Intelligence
- **Target Audience:** Software engineers, fintech architects, payment operations teams, and CFOs
- **Personality:** Analytical, deterministic, transparent, developer-centric, trustworthy

## 2. Digital Infrastructure Destinations

> **Note:** The following domains represent intended deployment destinations and architecture mapping; they are not active in Stage 1.

| Component | Target URL | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 3. Visual Identity

### Color Palette

- **Midnight Navy (`#0B0F19` / `#111827`)**: Base canvas representing stability, institutional fintech rigor, and deep technical authority.
- **Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4` / `#22D3EE`)**: Primary accent signaling intelligence, high precision, speed, and modern developer tooling.
- **Slate & Neutral Tones (`#64748B` / `#94A3B8` / `#F8FAFC`)**: Functional backgrounds, secondary text, and borders for high readability and low visual fatigue.
- **Emerald Green (`#10B981`)**: Success, verified reconciliation, net credit settlements.
- **Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`)**: Calculation estimates, pending settlement clearance, advisory warnings.
- **Crimson Red (`#EF4444`)**: Reconciliation discrepancies, boundary validation errors, chargebacks.

### Typography

- **Headings & Brand:** Modern geometric sans-serif (Inter, Geist Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans)
- **Data & Numeric Displays:** High-legibility tabular monospaced numerals (Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono)
- **Prose:** Neutral, crisp body copy optimized for documentation read-flow

## 4. Brand Tone and Voice

- **Precise & Mathematical:** We speak in exact terms—minor currency units, basis points, round-half-up, and deterministic reconciliation.
- **No Exaggerated Claims:** We do not claim to be a bank, an acquirer, or an infallible financial advisor. We are developer-first infrastructure providing settlement intelligence.
- **Data Privacy First:** We celebrate developer-owned payment data and transparent arithmetic over closed proprietary black boxes.
